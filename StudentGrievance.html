Welcome to the Student Grievance Redressal Portal. This portal provides students with information about submitting complaints and resolving their concerns.

About Us

The Student Grievance Redressal Cell aims to provide a platform where students can express their concerns regarding academic and non-academic issues.

Our objective is to ensure a fair, transparent and student-friendly environment.

Grievance Categories

Students can report issues related to the following categories:

  • Academic Issues
  • Faculty Related Issues
  • Examination Issues
  • Hostel Issues
  • Transportation Issues
  • Infrastructure Issues
  • Library Issues
  • Fee Related Issues
  • Other Issues

Grievance Submission Process

  1. Identify the issue you want to report.
  2. Select the appropriate grievance category.
  3. Provide the necessary details.
  4. Submit your grievance to the concerned department.
  5. Wait for the grievance to be reviewed.
  6. Check the resolution status.
